Zapopan — Operations & Data Improvement

Using analytics, automation, and stakeholder management to improve program performance at scale.

The Challenge

Managing large-scale public programs required coordinating thousands of participants, multiple stakeholders, and extensive operational processes.

My Role

I developed dashboards, automated administrative workflows, and analyzed participant behavior to improve operational outcomes.

Impact

  • 3,000+ participants supported
  • 30+ partnerships coordinated
  • Processing reduced from one week to under ten minutes
  • Dropout reduced from 40% to 5%

Solution

Combining data analysis, automation, and stakeholder engagement enabled more efficient operations and improved participant retention.

Key Takeaway

Technology creates the most value when it is aligned with operational realities and the needs of the people using it.
