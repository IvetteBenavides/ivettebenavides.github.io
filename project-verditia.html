Verditia — AI Consulting & Workflow Analysis

Diagnosing operational challenges and identifying practical AI opportunities for a growing retail business.

The Challenge

Verditia was operating across physical stores and e-commerce channels, but many critical processes depended on manual coordination and fragmented information.

Inventory visibility, customer follow-up, and sales reporting lacked a centralized structure, limiting scalability.

My Role

I analyzed business workflows, identified operational bottlenecks, and designed an AI roadmap aligned with the company's priorities and data maturity.

Approach

  • Mapped operational workflows
  • Analyzed inventory and sales processes
  • Identified data quality challenges
  • Prioritized AI opportunities by business value

Solution

The outcome was a phased AI roadmap including inventory forecasting, catalog synchronization, personalized recommendations, automated customer follow-up, and centralized reporting.

Key Takeaway

Successful AI initiatives start with understanding business processes and data quality, not with selecting technology.
